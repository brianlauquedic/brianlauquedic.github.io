    <h1>A playbook built for the way crypto moves.</h1>
    <p class="lede">Seven steps from brief to breakthrough &mdash; a repeatable, transparent process engineered for the speed, scrutiny and narrative density of Web3 news cycles.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section playbook-page">
  <div class="container">

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">01</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Discovery &amp; narrative audit</h3>
        <p>Before we touch a single press release, we map the ground. Who you serve, how you&rsquo;re perceived today, what stories your competitors already own, and which narratives have momentum in the outlets that matter to your audience.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Stakeholder interviews across founders, product, investors and community</li>
          <li>Coverage audit &mdash; what&rsquo;s been said about you and about the category</li>
          <li>Narrative landscape map &mdash; adjacent players, narrative white space, risks</li>
          <li>Audience &amp; journalist overlap analysis</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">02</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Positioning &amp; messaging</h3>
        <p>We translate discovery into a sharp, defensible position &mdash; then a messaging system the whole team can use without sounding like a press release.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Core positioning statement (one sentence, defensible against competitors)</li>
          <li>Message pillars &mdash; the three or four story arcs we&rsquo;ll play for the year</li>
          <li>Proof point bank &mdash; the data, quotes and references journalists actually need</li>
          <li>Tone of voice guide &mdash; how you sound in a press release vs. on X vs. in an op-ed</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">03</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Media &amp; KOL mapping</h3>
        <p>Tiered lists of outlets, reporters and creators matched to your narrative &mdash; not generic template lists. We score each target on beat fit, recency of relevant coverage, audience overlap and our ability to get a response.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Tier-1, tier-2 and niche trade publications, with beat owner per outlet</li>
          <li>KOL shortlist across X, YouTube and regional platforms &mdash; vetted for engagement quality</li>
          <li>Analyst and podcast target list</li>
          <li>Embargo and exclusive relationship map for major moments</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">04</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Story engineering</h3>
        <p>We build the calendar of announcements, op-eds, data stories, interviews and timely newsjacks that turns positioning into sustained coverage &mdash; designed around your product roadmap, not the agency&rsquo;s billing cycle.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Announcement pipeline &mdash; launches, upgrades, partnerships, milestones</li>
          <li>Thought leadership program &mdash; bylines, op-eds and interviews</li>
          <li>Data journalism &mdash; turning your on-chain and product data into news</li>
          <li>Newsjacking framework &mdash; what we chase, what we skip, within hours</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">05</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Outreach &amp; placement</h3>
        <p>Direct pitching to mapped reporters, embargoed rollouts, exclusives with tier-1 outlets, and Chainwire / PR distribution where wire coverage compounds rather than competes with earned media.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Personalised pitches written to each reporter&rsquo;s recent beat</li>
          <li>Embargoed launches, coordinated across time zones</li>
          <li>Exclusive placements with the outlets whose audience matters most</li>
          <li>Wire services for broad reach, used as amplification not substitute</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">06</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Amplification</h3>
        <p>Every earned win is an input to the next campaign. KOLs, social, community and paid layers compound the coverage so one tier-1 placement becomes a week of visibility.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>KOL activation calendars synced with press hits</li>
          <li>Owned-channel content packs &mdash; X threads, LinkedIn, newsletter</li>
          <li>Community briefings for Discord, Telegram and regional channels</li>
          <li>Paid amplification where the earned signal justifies the spend</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="playbook-page__step">
      <div class="playbook-page__step-num">07</div>
      <div class="playbook-page__step-body">
        <h3>Measurement &amp; iteration</h3>
        <p>Coverage counts are a starting point, not an answer. We report on placements, reach, sentiment, share of voice, and the downstream signals &mdash; pipeline, partnerships, talent inbound &mdash; that tell us whether the program is doing its real job.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Monthly dashboards with placements, reach, sentiment and SOV</li>
          <li>Quarterly narrative review &mdash; what&rsquo;s working, what to retire</li>
          <li>Attribution conversations with sales, product and talent teams</li>
          <li>Iterative playbook adjustments &mdash; this document is never frozen</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>
