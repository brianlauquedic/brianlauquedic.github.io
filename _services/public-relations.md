**Tier-1 media placements that give your brand authority and attention in the crypto press ecosystem.**

Coverage is a byproduct of narrative. We start by sharpening the story — who you serve, why you exist, what you move the market toward — and then engineer the announcements, data, interviews and commentaries that give reporters a reason to pick up the phone.

For exchanges, protocols and public-markets-adjacent businesses, we handle the full comms stack: from pre-launch positioning and embargoed exclusives to reputation defense during volatile news cycles. Our team reads the same Twitter, Telegram and Substack you do — and writes to reporters who are already paying attention.

Engagements range from launch pushes to always-on retainers, delivered with the same playbook we apply to tier-1 exchanges.
