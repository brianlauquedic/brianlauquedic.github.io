Exchanges are the most scrutinized brands in crypto. Every announcement &mdash; a new listing, a wallet upgrade, a fee change &mdash; is parsed by traders, journalists, regulators and competitors within minutes. The communications bar is the highest in the industry, and the margin for error is the smallest.

We&rsquo;ve built our reputation working alongside the venues that set those standards. The same disciplines apply across the trading stack: market makers managing inventory and reputational risk, perps platforms competing on uptime and depth, and DEX teams translating on-chain proof points into mainstream credibility.

Our work for this category covers product launches, listing announcements, regulatory milestones, security incidents and the long-running brand programs that keep top-tier venues differentiated in a category where users compare on fees, depth and trust &mdash; in that order.
