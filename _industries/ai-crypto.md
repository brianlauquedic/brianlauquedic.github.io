The AI &times; crypto category has compressed years of narrative formation into months. Agent platforms, decentralized compute, data networks and DePIN protocols are competing for the same investor attention, the same press calendar and the same KOL bandwidth &mdash; while the technology underneath them is still being defined in real time.

Our work in this category centers on positioning that survives the hype cycle: distinguishing real distributed-systems work from rebranded crypto plays, framing agent capabilities honestly enough to earn analyst coverage, and building the editorial relationships that decide which projects get seen as serious AI infrastructure versus which get bucketed as memecoin season.

We support founders who are building infrastructure for autonomous agents, decentralized model training, verifiable inference, GPU networks and the broader DePIN economy.
