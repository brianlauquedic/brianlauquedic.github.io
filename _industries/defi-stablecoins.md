DeFi communications run on a different clock and a different language than traditional fintech. Protocol updates, governance votes, integration announcements and security disclosures all need to move at the speed of on-chain activity &mdash; while remaining accurate enough to survive a forensic Twitter thread an hour later.

We support lending markets, DEXs, yield platforms, stablecoin issuers and the new wave of RWA-backed credit protocols. Programs typically combine technical PR (audits, integrations, governance), institutional outreach (custodians, market makers, treasury teams) and end-user marketing across X, Discord and Telegram.

Compliance-aware messaging is non-negotiable in this category. Regulatory scrutiny across the US, EU and Asia means every product claim and every yield number gets filtered through a legal lens before it ships &mdash; and good communications make that filtering invisible to the audience.
